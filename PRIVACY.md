# Handur Tkt — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Handur Tkt ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner with the owner's own TikTok account.

## Data the app accesses
- **Basic profile info** (`user.info.basic`): the owner's TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
- **Access tokens** from TikTok, used only to upload drafts to the owner's account.
- **Videos** created by the owner on their own computer, uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.

## How data is stored
- Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not shared.
- The app has no server and no database. It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
- The app does not access data of any other TikTok user.

## Retention and deletion
The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services) and delete the local token file.
