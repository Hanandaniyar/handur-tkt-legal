## What the app does
- Lets the owner log in with TikTok (Login Kit) to identify the owner's account.
- Uploads short videos created by the owner to the owner's own TikTok inbox as **drafts** (Content Posting API, Upload).
- The owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it. The app never publishes content publicly by itself.
